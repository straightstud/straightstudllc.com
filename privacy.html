Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective date: July 18, 2026
Last updated: July 18, 2026
Business: Straight Stud Construction LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”)

This policy describes practices for our website and related customer communications. It is a template for a small construction business website and is not legal advice. Have an attorney review it for Michigan law and your specific operations if needed.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and city/neighborhood
  • Project details and messages you send through our contact form or email
  • Any files or photos you choose to attach when corresponding with us

We may also receive limited technical data automatically when you visit our site, such as browser type, device type, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, and referral source — typically via our hosting provider or analytics tools if enabled.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to quote requests and customer service inquiries
  • Schedule consultations, estimates, and project work
  • Communicate about proposals, contracts, invoices, and warranties
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We may share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site or business (for example, form delivery services such as Formspree, email providers, or web hosts), under obligations to protect your data
  • Professional advisors such as insurance carriers, accountants, or attorneys when reasonably necessary
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect safety, property, or legal rights

If we use subcontractors or suppliers on your project, we may share relevant project details as needed to perform the work.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our site may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for basic functionality and security. If we later add analytics or advertising cookies, we will update this policy and, where required, provide notice or consent options.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information you submit (for example, transmitting the site over HTTPS when hosted properly, limiting access to business email, and using reputable form providers). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please avoid sending sensitive financial account numbers or government ID numbers through the website contact form.

6. Data Retention

We retain inquiry and project-related information for as long as needed to respond to you, manage projects, meet legal/tax/insurance requirements, or resolve disputes — then delete or de-identify it when no longer needed, subject to backup systems.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access or correct personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to delete certain information (subject to legal retention needs)
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing emails (project-related messages may still be sent as needed)

To make a request, email request@straightstudllc.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is directed to adults seeking construction services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
