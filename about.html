Straight Stud Construction LLC is a Greater Grand Rapids–area construction company focused on custom decks, home additions, exterior upgrades, and custom homes.

We started with a simple standard: do the work right, communicate clearly, and leave every job site better than we found it. That approach has guided every project since 2021.

Whether you need a new outdoor living space, more square footage, refreshed siding, or a full custom build, we bring the same attention to structure, materials, and finish.
